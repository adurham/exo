**The Observer Pattern**  
The observer pattern is a behavioral design pattern that defines a one-to-many dependency between objects. When one object (the subject) changes state, all its dependents (observers) are notified and updated automatically. This pattern is fundamental to event-driven programming, user interfaces, and distributed systems.
