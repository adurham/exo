## 4. Ruled out by measurement or source read

- **Architecture/config:** 1017 and the Sept checkpoint have byte-identical
  architectural fields (`config.json` for both, pulled from the Hub): `index_topk`
  512, `candidate_topk_blocks` 2048, `candidate_block_size` 8, `compress_ratios`,
  `kv_source_layer_ids`, `index_source_layer_ids`, 40 layers, 64 heads, 1 KV head.
- **Quantization layout of the indexer:** both checkpoints store
  `layers.N.attn.indexer.{wk,wq_b}` as trellis + `mul1`/`suh`/`svh`. Identical.
- **`hier geometry` strip numbers** (13/4 on 1017 vs 22-38 on Sept) are a boot-order
  artifact: the line is emitted per distinct `(bsz, n, …)` on a hier-gated forward
  (`n > _FENCE_MIN_ROWS = 16`), so decode (n=1) never touches it. The Sept-era log
  shows the same 13 and 4 lines at other row counts.
- **Code changes** `b88d65dd3304..a8731f17e`: nothing touches attention, KV/session
  cache, park store, indexer or the speculative round structure. mlx pin identical
  at both ends; the two mlx-lm bumps are a 36-line load-time weight-slicing fix.
- **Measurement artifact:** engine `obs` turn-end/first-token lines agree with the
  client within 1%.

## 5. The machine-state incident (open)

At 13:41/13:48 both Studios were rebooted (`./reboot-node.sh`, FileVault
authrestart) to test whether box state explained the decode loss. Since that boot,
decode collapsed **at every depth**, not just deep context, and it did not recover.

Decode vs prompt length on the post-reboot boot (same body, only the prefix length
varies; `out-sweep/`):

| prompt tokens | 26 | 84 | 294 | 602 | 798 | 1,003 | 10K | 50K | 100K | 300K |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| decode tok/s | 25.9 | 15.2 | 8.0 | 5.6 | 6.7 | 4.9 | 5.3 | 5.3 | 5.5 | 3.8 |

Same binary, same weights, same launcher did 26.5 tok/s at 1K on the pre-reboot boot.

Excluded on the post-reboot boot: paging/compression (all counters flat during
prefill and decode), disk I/O (`iostat` idle), host CPU (a 20M-iteration Python loop
runs 0.64 s here vs 0.78 s on the M4 Max laptop), GPU clocks (`powermetrics`: 1570-1577
MHz, i.e. max), contention (one API POST per row), thermal warnings (none recorded),
and the post-boot `audiomxd`/`configd` churn (killed both — decode unchanged, 4.81
tok/s at 1K).

The signature is a **GPU stalled on submission**: max clock, ~90% "active residency",
but only ~7 W, with host threads parked in `Fence::wait` / `__psynch_cvwait`. Prefill
is unaffected because it makes few large submits; decode makes hundreds of small ones
per token. A warm reboot did not clear it. Next step is a cold power cycle; if that
does not clear it, the fallback is re-staging the Sept checkpoint to restore service
and to serve as the control for the §3 comparison.
