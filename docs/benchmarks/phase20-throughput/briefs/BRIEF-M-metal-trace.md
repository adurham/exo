## Why
The ~40% (~55-60 ms) of the decode round that the wall model cannot explain is the whole decode campaign. A host timer (Phase 1) attributes host-visible segments; a Metal System Trace gives the **GPU's own kernel/command-buffer timeline** (busy vs idle gaps, command-buffer cadence, comm-induced bubbles) from outside the process, with no code change and no relaunch. This is the plan's Fallback-B tool for 0d and the independent cross-check of Phase 1. The PM will run it live on a node during a decode window; you build and validate the tooling OFFLINE.

* The decode round is ~150 ms; one round = draft + verify forward (40 layers, TP2 collectives per layer) + deferred rollback/append_ctx; per-layer comm is likely visible as GPU idle gaps. Target analysis output: GPU-busy fraction over a window, command-buffer/encoder durations and counts, idle-gap histogram (>50 us), and a heuristic segmentation of the timeline into "rounds" (periodic large gaps / commit cadence) with per-round busy ms and gap ms, plus the top-10 longest GPU intervals by label.
