# paperclip [list]
PROMPT: List ten genuinely different uses for a paperclip.

1. **Bookmark** – Clip it onto the edge of a page to mark your place.  
2. **Zipper pull** – Thread it through a broken zipper tab as a temporary handle.  
3. **SIM tray ejector** – Straighten it and use the tip to pop open a phone’s SIM slot.  
4. **Reset button presser** – Use the tip to press tiny recessed reset buttons on routers or gadgets.  
5. **Chip bag clip** – Fold the top of a snack bag and clip it shut.  
6. **Cable organizer** – Wrap it around a coiled cord to keep it tidy.  
7. **Temporary S-hook** – Bend it into a hook for lightweight ornaments or keys.  
8. **Makeshift key ring** – Bend it into a loop to hold a few keys together.  
9. **Mini screwdriver** – Use the flattened tip on eyeglass or toy-battery compartment screws.  
10. **Knitting stitch marker** – Clip onto yarn to mark a stitch or row, like a safety pin.
