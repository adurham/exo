# zh_autumn [multilingual]
PROMPT: 先用一句中文描写秋雨的声音，然后用英文解释这句话的意思。(Write one Chinese sentence describing the sound of autumn rain, then explain it in English.)

秋雨淅淅沥沥地落在枯黄的梧桐叶上，像无数细碎的叹息。

English explanation: This sentence describes the sound of autumn rain as a soft, continuous pattering (“淅淅沥沥”) on withered yellow plane leaves, comparing it to countless tiny sighs. It conveys a cool, quiet, and slightly melancholic autumn atmosphere.
