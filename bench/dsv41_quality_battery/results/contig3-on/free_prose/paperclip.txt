# paperclip [list]
PROMPT: List ten genuinely different uses for a paperclip.

1. **Bookmark** – clip it over a page to mark your place.  
2. **Zipper pull** – loop it through a broken zipper slider to create a new grip.  
3. **Reset pin** – straighten one end to press recessed reset buttons on routers, cameras, etc.  
4. **SIM ejector** – straighten it and push into a phone’s SIM tray pinhole.  
5. **Cable organizer** – clip several charging cords together to reduce tangling.  
6. **Bag clip** – fold and clip a chip or cereal bag shut.  
7. **Ornament hook** – bend it into an S-shape to hang lightweight ornaments.  
8. **Plant tie** – straighten and loosely twist it around a stem and support stick.  
9. **Touchscreen stylus** – hold the metal clip and use the tip to tap a capacitive screen.  
10. **Temporary eyeglass hinge pin** – if a hinge screw falls out, thread a snipped paperclip through the hinge as a stopgap.
