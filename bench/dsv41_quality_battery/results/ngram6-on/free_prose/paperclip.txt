# paperclip [list]
PROMPT: List ten genuinely different uses for a paperclip.

1. **Bookmark** – Clip it onto a page to mark your place.
2. **Zipper pull replacement** – Loop it through a broken zipper slider to give yourself a grab tab.
3. **Reset pin** – Straighten it to press a recessed reset button on a router or gadget.
4. **Cable organizer** – Clip charging cords together to reduce tangling.
5. **Bag clip** – Fold a chip or cereal bag and clip it shut.
6. **Ornament hook** – Bend it into an S-hook to hang a lightweight decoration.
7. **Plant tie** – Straighten and twist it around a stem and stake for support.
8. **Touchscreen stylus** – Hold the metal clip and use the tip on a capacitive screen.
9. **Eyeglass hinge pin** – Use a short straightened piece as a temporary hinge pin if a screw falls out.
10. **Key ring** – Bend it into a loop and thread keys onto it.
