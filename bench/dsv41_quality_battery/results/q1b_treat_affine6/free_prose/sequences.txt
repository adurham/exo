[2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, ...]  
[a, c, e, g, i, k, m, ...]  
[1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, ...]

Reasoning: The first doubles each time, so it is the powers of 2. The second takes every other letter of the alphabet, giving the odd-positioned letters. The third is the Fibonacci sequence, where each new term is the sum of the two previous terms.
