# paperclip [list]
PROMPT: List ten genuinely different uses for a paperclip.

1. **Bookmark** – slide it over the edge of a page to mark your place.  
2. **Zipper pull replacement** – loop it through a broken zipper slider to make a temporary handle.  
3. **SIM-card tray ejector / reset pin** – straighten it to pop open a phone’s SIM tray or press a tiny reset button.  
4. **Cable organizer** – wrap it around charging cords to keep them from tangling.  
5. **Chip-bag clip** – fold the top of a snack bag and clip it shut.  
6. **S-hook** – bend it into a hook to hang lightweight ornaments or keys.  
7. **Key ring** – bend it into a loop to hold a few spare keys.  
8. **Earring hook** – shape it into a simple French hook for a homemade pendant.  
9. **Eyeglass hinge pin** – use a short piece as a temporary replacement if a tiny hinge screw falls out.  
10. **Magnetized compass needle** – rub it on a magnet, float it on a leaf, and it will point north.
