# lighthouse [continuation]
PROMPT: Continue this story in one vivid paragraph: 'The lighthouse keeper noticed the light was not his own.'

The lighthouse keeper noticed the light was not his own. It swept the sea in slow, deliberate arcs—three silver pulses, a pause, then three again—while his own beam, still burning behind him, shrank to a trembling amber halo. He climbed the spiral stairs, boots ringing on cold iron, and found the great lens turning though no clockwork drove it; frost bloomed across the brass, and in the glass a second light, salt-white and impossibly far, answered from beyond the reef where no lighthouse stood. Below, the tide began arranging stones into letters, and the keeper understood that something out there had learned his language—and was calling him home.
