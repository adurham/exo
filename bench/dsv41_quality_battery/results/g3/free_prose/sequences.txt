2. `[a, c, e, g, i, k, m, ...]`
3. `[1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, ...]`

Reasoning: The first sequence doubles each term, so each number is twice the one before it. The second sequence uses every other letter of the alphabet, starting at `a` and skipping one letter each time. The third sequence is the Fibonacci sequence: after the first two `1`s, each term is the sum of the two previous terms, so `1+1=2`, `1+2=3`, `2+3=5`, and so on.
