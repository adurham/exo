# hash_table [technical]
PROMPT: Explain how a hash table works, in plain language, as if to a curious teenager.

Imagine a giant wall of lockers, numbered 0 to 999. You need to store stuff by name, like “Maya’s backpack.” Instead of dumping it anywhere and searching every locker later, you make a rule: turn a name into a locker number. For ejemplo, add up the letters’ positions and take the last 3 digits, so “Maya” might become locker 472. You put Maya’s stuff in locker 472. Later, to find it, you do the same math and walk straight to 472. That rule that turns a key into a locker number is called a **hash function**. The lockers are called **buckets**.
