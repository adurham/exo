# paperclip [list]
PROMPT: List ten genuinely different uses for a paperclip.

1. **Bookmark** – Clip it over a page to hold your reading place.  
2. **Zipper pull**– Thread it through a broken zipper slider as an emergency tab.  
3. **Reset pin** – Straighten it to press recessed resetbuttons on routers or electronics.  
4. **SIM ejector**– Straighten and push into a phone’s SIM-tray pinhole.  
5. **Cable organizer**– Clip charging cords together to reduce tangling.  
6. **Bag clip**– Fold and clip a chip or cereal bag shut.  
7. **Ornament hook**– Bend into an S-hook to hang light decorations.  
8. **Plant tie**– Straighten and loosely twist around a stem for support.  
9. **Touchscreen stylus*– Hold the metal clip and use the tip on a capacitive screen.  
10. **Temporary eyeglasses hinge pin** – If a hinge screw falls out, trim and insert a paperclip wire as a stand-in.
