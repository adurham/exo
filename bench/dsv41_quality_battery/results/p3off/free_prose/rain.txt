# rain [prose]
PROMPT: Write a short paragraph about the sound of rain on a metal roof.

The first drops land like scattered applause, each one a sharp, bright ping against the tin. Then the sky opens and the rhythm thickens into a steady drumroll, a thousand tiny hammers striking in wild, overlapping time. The sound fills the whole house—a roaring lullaby that drowns out thought, wraps around you like a blanket, and turns the simplest shelter into a fortress against the storm.
