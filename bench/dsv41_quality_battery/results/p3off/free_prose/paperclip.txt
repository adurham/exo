# paperclip [list]
PROMPT: List ten genuinely different uses for a paperclip.

1. **Bookmark** – Slide it over a page to mark your place in a book or notebook.
2. **Zipper pull** – Thread it through a broken zipper slider to make a temporary pull tab.
3. **SIM tray ejector** – Straighten one end and use it to pop open a phone’s SIM card tray.
4. **Reset-button presser** – Use the tip to press a recessed reset button on a router or small electronic device.
5. **Cable organizer** – Clip around bundled charging cords to keep them tidy, or wrap it around a cable as a label.
6. **Bag clip** – Fold the top of a chip or snack bag and clip it shut.
7. **Money clip** – Bend it into a U-shape to hold folded bills or a few cards.
8. **Ornament hook** – Bend it into a small S-hook to hang lightweight ornaments or tags.
9. **Touchscreen stylus** – Hold the metal clip and use the tip on a capacitive touchscreen for more precise tapping.
10. **Keychain loop** – Bend it into a loop and use it to attach a key to a belt loop or bag strap.
