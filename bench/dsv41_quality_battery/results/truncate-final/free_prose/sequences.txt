# sequences [continuation]
PROMPT: Complete these sequences and then explain your reasoning in prose: [2,4,8,16, ...], [a,c,e,g, ...], [1,1,2,3,5, ...].

[2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, ...]  
[a, c, e, g, i, k, ...]  
[1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, ...]

Reasoning:  
The first sequence doubles each term: 2 × 2 = 4, 4 × 2 = 8, and so on.  
The second sequence lists every other letter of the alphabet: a, c, e, g, i, k, etc.  
The third sequence is the Fibonacci sequence: each term after the first two is the sum of the two preceding terms, e.g. 1 + 1 = 2, 1 + 2 = 3, 2 + 3 = 5, and so on.
