# zh_autumn [multilingual]
PROMPT: 先用一句中文描写秋雨的声音，然后用英文解释这句话的意思。(Write one Chinese sentence describing the sound of autumn rain, then explain it in English.)

中文：秋雨淅淅沥沥，像无数细小的指尖轻敲着枯黄的梧桐叶。

English: This sentence describes the sound of autumn rain as a soft, continuous pitter-patter, comparing it to countless tiny fingertips gently tapping withered yellow plane-tree leaves. “淅淅沥沥” is an onomatopoeic phrase for light, steady rain, and the withered leaves help evoke the quiet, melancholy mood of autumn.
