The lighthouse keeper noticed the light was not his own. It came from the sea where no shore belonged, a bone-white beam sweeping slow and wrong, three short pulses, one long, as if answering a rhythm he had never taught anyone. The cold glare slid across the black water, touched the tower’s windows, and for a heartbeat every pane held a pale, salt-drenched copy of him, each mouthing a warning he could not hear. His coffee went cold in his hand; the logbook fell open, and the ink was already wet though he had not yet written a word.”
