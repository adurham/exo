# paperclip [list]
PROMPT: List ten genuinely different uses for a paperclip.

1. **Bookmark** – clip it over the page edge to mark your place.  
2. **Zipper pull replacement** – loop it through a broken zipper slider.  
3. **Reset-button tool** – straighten it to press a recessed reset button on electronics.  
4. **SIM/microSD ejector** – straighten one end to push out a card tray.  
5. **Cable/bag twist tie** – wrap it around cords or a bread bag to keep things closed.  
6. **S-hook** – bend it into a hook to hang lightweight ornaments or keys.  
7. **Plant tie** – secure a stem to a stake or trellis.  
8. **Mini screwdriver** – use the tip to tighten tiny eyeglass screws.  
9. **Nail/cranny cleaner** – scrape under fingernails or clean small crevices like keyboard gaps.  
10. **Improvised lock pick** – bend the tip to rake or poke a basic pin-tumbler lock.
