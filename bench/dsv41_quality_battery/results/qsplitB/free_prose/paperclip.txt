# paperclip [list]
PROMPT: List ten genuinely different uses for a paperclip.

1. **SIM-card tray ejector** — straighten it and push into the tiny pinhole to pop out the tray.  
2. **Reset-button tool** — use the tip to reach recessed reset buttons on routers, cameras, or gadgets.  
3. **Emergency zipper pull** — loop it through a broken zipper slider to make a temporary tab.  
4. **Cable organizer** — bend it around a coiled cord to keep headphones or charging cables tidy.  
5. **Temporary S-hook** — bend into an S shape to hang lightweight keys, ornaments, or tags.  
6. **Tiny crevice cleaner** — use it to dig lint/dust from keyboard gaps, phone ports, and seams.  
7. **Circuit jumper/test probe** — use it as a conductive bridge on a breadboard or to test contacts.  
8. **Makeshift lock pick/tension wrench** — bend it to help pick simple pin-tumbler locks.  
9. **Plant tie** — bend around a stem and stake to support a leaning seedling.  
10. **Backup earring hook** — bend into a small hook for a lightweight earring or pendant.
